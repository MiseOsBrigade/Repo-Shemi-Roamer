# MiseOS LinkForge Brand System

**Promise:** Turn a link into a traceable, evidence-aware knowledge packet.

**Tagline:** Save the source. Trace the truth. Ship the packet.

The interlocking links represent source capture and synthesis. The check mark
represents verification. Forest green anchors the system in GoodShyt Systems;
teal signals research, orange signals action, and gold marks verified state.

Use Sora or Manrope for headlines, Inter or DM Sans for body copy, and Space
Mono for hashes, IDs, timestamps, and provenance metadata.
