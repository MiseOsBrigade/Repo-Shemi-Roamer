# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MiseOS welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, experience, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, accept constructive feedback, protect private information, and prioritize community safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1. See <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
